Privacy Policy — Hermes Discord Bridge

Last updated: 2026-08-08

This bot is a personal assistant bridge that connects the operator's Discord account to a local Hermes AI agent instance.

Data we collect

  • Messages you send to the bot (including their text content).
  • Your Discord user ID and username.
  • The server and channel IDs where the bot is used.

How we use it

Solely to process your request through the Hermes agent and return a response.

Storage

Messages are processed by the local Hermes instance and may be logged locally for session continuity and memory. They are not transmitted to third parties except the AI model provider that Hermes is configured to use.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party.

Retention

Logs may be retained locally on the operator's machine. Contact the operator to request deletion of your data.

Contact

James — reachable via the Discord server where the bot operates.
